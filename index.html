Who we are

One chamber for every business of Kutch, from a small shop in Bhuj market to the biggest industries at Mundra and Kandla. We bring experts to Bhuj, coordinating with relevant offices regarding the issues of our members, and speaking for business before the government.

Kutch is India's largest district and one of its fastest growing. BCCIF makes sure the businesses that build it are heard.

From the President & Founder

Anil H. Gor

President & Founder, Bhuj Chamber of Commerce & Industries Federation

Shri Anil H. Gor, President and Founder of the Bhuj Chamber of Commerce & Industries Federation, is a well known name in the business and the entertainment sectors of Kachchh. A visionary leader, he is committed to represent the local business community and building cooperation among traders, entrepreneurs, industries and all stakeholders of Kachchh district.

He is the Chairman of the Global Kachchh Federation and the Kutch Cinema Exhibitors Association, a trustee of Akhil Kachchh Rajgor Mahasabha, and the Managing Trustee of the Rajgor Harishankar Devji Ajani Foundation Trust. He also served as President of the Rotary Club of Bhuj Flamingo in 2012–13, reflecting his work in both business and community service.

With a focus on regional development, business networking and collective progress, he works to strengthen the voice of Kachchh’s business community, promote unity among its stakeholders and create new opportunities for sustainable economic growth. His strong voice, heard on many regional and international platforms, reflects his determination to secure for the business fraternity of Kachchh the full rights and privileges set out in the Federation’s objectives.

Business in Kutch

Where Kutch business is growing

01 / 06
01PORTS

Gateway to the world

Mundra and Deendayal (Kandla) are among India's busiest ports, handling a large share of the country's cargo.

02ENERGY

Sun & wind power

The Khavda renewable energy park in the Rann is planned to be one of the largest in the world.

03SALT

Salt of the nation

Kutch produces most of India's salt, from the pans of the Rann and the coast.

04CRAFTS

Hands that make history

Embroidery, Ajrakh, Bhujodi weaving and Rogan art, crafts that support thousands of families.

05CEMENT & MINERALS

Built on stone

Limestone, bentonite, bauxite and lignite make Kutch a major centre for cement and mineral industries.
