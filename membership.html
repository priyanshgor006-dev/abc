Membership

Join the Bhuj Chamber of Commerce & Industries Federation and become part of Kutch’s business community.

About membership

What it means to be a member

The Bhuj Chamber of Commerce & Industries Federation (BCCIF) is the common platform of traders, industries, professionals and trade associations of Bhuj and Kutch. It was founded with the support of 51 trade associations, and works to protect and grow the business interests of the region.

When you become a member, your business becomes part of this federation. You get a voice in the matters that affect trade and industry, a network of fellow business owners, and the support of the Chamber when you need it.

Membership is available for every size of business, from small traders and startups to large industries, as well as trade associations and institutions. Choose the category that fits your business from the fee table below.

Who can become a member?

  • Private and public limited companies
  • Industries of every size (minerals, import–export, logistics)
  • Proprietorships, partnerships and LLPs
  • Traders and shop owners
  • Startups and young entrepreneurs
  • Trade associations
  • Institutions, trusts and NGOs
Why join

Benefits of becoming a member

A voice for your business

The Chamber takes members’ issues to the government, local authorities and other bodies, and works for policies that help trade and industry in Kutch.

Networking

Meet business owners, industry leaders and the 51 trade associations connected with the Chamber.

Events and seminars

Invitations to the Chamber’s meetings, seminars, workshops and business events.

Information and updates

Updates on government schemes, circulars, notifications and changes that affect your business.

Guidance and support

Help and guidance from the Chamber office when you face a business problem or need the right contact.

Recognition

Be part of the official business body of Kutch, and grow your business’s standing in the community.

Membership Application Form

Fill in the official form, send it to the Chamber by email and by courier, and wait for the Chamber’s decision. Pay the fee only after your application is approved.

Step 1 – Download and fill in the form

Download the official BCCIF membership application form (PDF), print it and fill in all the details. Sign it and put your company stamp where marked.

Download membership form (PDF)

Step 2 – Email the soft copy

Scan or take clear photos of the filled form and the documents below, and email them together to:

ccibbhuj@gmail.com

Please write “Membership Application – your firm name” in the email subject.

Documents required:

  • Filled and signed membership form
  • Certificate
  • PAN Card
  • Registration Certificate
  • GST / Registration Certificate
  • Address Proof
  • Partnership Deed / MOA / AOA
  • Passport Size Photograph

Renew your membership

Membership is renewed every year on the date you joined. For example, if you joined on 10 August, your renewal is due on 10 August each year.

Step 1 – Pay the annual fee

Pay the annual fee for your membership category (as per the fee table above, plus GST) to the Chamber’s bank account shown above, on or before your renewal date.

Step 2 – Email the payment receipt

Email the payment receipt or transaction (UTR) details to:

ccibbhuj@gmail.com

Please write “Membership Renewal – your firm name – Membership No.” in the email subject.

Open email to send receipt

Step 3 – Confirmation

The Chamber office will confirm your renewal, and your membership continues for one more year.

Patron and Lifetime members do not need to renew.
